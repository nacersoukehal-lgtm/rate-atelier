Privacy

Rate Atelier stores preferences, watchlists, and alert settings in your browser. You can remove them by clearing the site’s data in your browser settings. Calculations run in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and your browser requests quotes, news, and other data from the providers listed on the data sources page. Those services may receive standard request information, such as your IP address.

Disclaimer

Rates and prices are for general information and may be delayed or incomplete. They should not be considered financial advice. Check the latest quote and fees directly with your provider before making a transaction.

Terms

Rate Atelier provides reference data and estimates. Availability depends on the hosting and data providers. Transfer, dealer, and trading prices can differ from the figures shown.

About our rates

Our currency converter uses published reference rates. In one USD-to-EUR comparison, Rate Atelier showed €652.46 for $725 while Google showed €656.02. The two sites used rates from different sources with different update schedules.

Your transfer provider may quote a different rate and charge fees. Enter the rate and fees from each provider to see what the recipient would get.

Privacy

Rate Atelier stores preferences, watchlists, and alert settings in your browser. You can remove them by clearing the site’s data in your browser settings. Calculations run in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and your browser requests quotes, news, and other data from the providers listed on the data sources page. Those services may receive standard request information, such as your IP address.

Rate Atelier uses Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit and how they find the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives information about your visit. Learn how Google uses this data or use the Analytics opt-out add-on.

Disclaimer

Rates and prices are for general information and may be delayed or incomplete. They should not be considered financial advice. Check the latest quote and fees directly with your provider before making a transaction.

Terms

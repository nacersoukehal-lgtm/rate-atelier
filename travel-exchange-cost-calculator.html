How fees are modeled

The exchanged principal is (total budget − fixed charges × number of transactions) ÷ (1 + percentage fee ÷ 100). The spendable amount is that principal × the quoted rate. Percentage charges are added to the principal, while all charges stay inside the same total budget.

Use the combined percentage charged on the exchanged principal, and include fixed charges from both your bank and the ATM operator in the sending currency. A quoted rate can already contain a spread: do not add the same spread again as a percentage fee. The shortfall compares your result with a fee-free reference conversion; it is not an extra charge.

This estimate excludes interest, cash-advance charges, minimum fees, and tiered pricing unless you include them in the inputs. Different providers may apply charges in a different order.

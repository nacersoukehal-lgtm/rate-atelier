How ECB reference rates work

The European Central Bank usually publishes euro reference rates around 16:00 Central European time on working days, except on TARGET closing days. The USD to EUR rate here is the inverse of the ECB’s USD per euro rate. These rates are published for information. Your bank or transfer service sets its own rate and fees. ECB exchange rate methodology

Single-date lookups use the latest prior rate in the preceding 30 days when the selected day has no observation. Date ranges include only rates published between the selected dates, inclusive. Reference rates come from Frankfurter.

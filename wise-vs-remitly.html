What to check in each quote

  • Wise. Wise says it uses the mid-market rate and shows the transfer fee upfront. The quoted fee can depend on the amount and how you pay. Wise transfer pricing.
  • Remitly. Check the rate and fee together, including whether a promotion applies. The rate can vary with the transfer and payment method. Remitly’s exchange-rate guide.

Compare delivery as well as payout

Get both quotes close together. Write down the quoted exchange rate, total fees, amount the recipient gets, and expected delivery time. If one quote uses cash pickup and the other uses a bank deposit, decide which delivery method fits before comparing the amounts.

Editorial Policy

Our guides aim to help readers understand calculations and market terminology without promising investment outcomes.

Our publishing standards

  • Write original explanations and attribute factual sources and quoted material.
  • Label worked examples as examples; do not present hypothetical rates as current quotes.
  • Distinguish observation dates, publishing dates, forecast figures, and historical results.
  • Avoid guaranteed returns, unsupported price predictions, and invented credentials or testimonials.
  • Link news headlines to their original publishers rather than republishing full articles.

Corrections

Report errors to rateatelier@gmail.com. Include the page, the statement or value, and a supporting source or screenshot. Corrections should fix the underlying calculation or source interpretation, not only its appearance.

Commercial relationships

Advertisements, sponsored material, and affiliate relationships should be identified where they appear. A listing or link does not itself constitute an endorsement. Comparisons should explain the information and assumptions used.

Financial information

Guides and calculators provide general information. They do not assess your circumstances or replace advice from an appropriately qualified professional. Check current rates, fees, and terms before acting.

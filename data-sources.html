Data Sources & Methodology

AboutData sourcesEditorial policyContact

Every quote should be read together with its instrument, unit, source, and observation date. Data may be delayed. These are reference figures, not executable dealer, broker, or transfer offers.

Sources by market

Frankfurter and the European Central Bank
Published currency reference rates. USD/EUR uses ECB observations through Frankfurter where available. Cross rates divide two rates with the same USD base; dates can differ, so check the displayed observations.
Standard Bullion
Gold, silver, platinum, and palladium. Quote cards use bid/ask midpoints in USD per troy ounce; historical points follow the provider’s series. A troy ounce is 31.1034768 grams.
Yahoo Finance
Stocks, indices, U.S. Dollar Index, and COMEX copper futures. Copper is quoted in USD per pound. Futures are distinct from physical metal and spot prices. Exchange delays and session times vary.
CoinPaprika and CoinGecko
Cryptocurrency prices, market capitalization, trading volume, and supplemental profiles. Provider coverage and methodology may differ.
Umicore, IMF, USGS, and specialty metal sources
Published benchmark observations with the stated grade, unit, and cadence. Monthly and annual observations can lag. They are not live spot quotes.
ShowMyTrades and named news publishers
Scheduled economic events and headlines retain their event or publication dates and link to their original sources.

Updates and saved data

The active dashboard checks FX and bullion about every minute, visible stock and crypto quotes about every five minutes, and calendar or news feeds about every fifteen minutes. Published snapshots are rebuilt by a scheduled GitHub workflow; scheduling and provider delays can affect freshness.

We keep provider observation times separate from the snapshot build time. If a live request fails, a saved or published observation can remain visible with its date. “Checking for updates” describes a request in progress; a session close and a stale quote have different meanings. A refresh does not guarantee that the provider has published anything newer.

Calculations and chart ranges

Percentage changes use the relevant unrounded reference values. Chart performance is measured between the first and last displayed observations, not necessarily calendar endpoints. Non-trading days may have no observation. Saved history must have adequate coverage for the selected range.

Melt values estimate metal content; they exclude dealer margins, fabrication premiums, taxes, and resale deductions. Transfer comparison uses the rates and fees entered by the visitor.

Report a data discrepancy with the instrument, unit, source, timestamp, and expected value.

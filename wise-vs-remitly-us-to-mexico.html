Wise vs Remitly to Mexico: how many pesos will arrive?

Compare the final MXN payout for the same total USD budget, then check that the delivery method works for your recipient. A bank deposit and a cash pickup are different services, even when the displayed payouts look similar.

Bank deposit or cash pickup comes first

Wise’s U.S.-to-Mexico page describes paying in USD and delivering MXN to a recipient’s bank account. Remitly’s Mexico page lists delivery options and partners for the route. Confirm what is available in your own quote; do not assume every option works for every recipient.

If cash pickup is essential, check the location, opening hours and collection requirements first. If the recipient has a suitable bank account, compare bank-deposit quotes from both providers on the same basis. Use the quoted arrival estimate for your chosen payment and delivery method rather than a general speed claim.

Check the first transfer and the next transfer separately

Remitly’s route page advertises a new-customer promotion with eligibility and amount conditions. Read the current terms in your quote. If you plan to send regularly, record both the welcome offer and the price available for a repeat transfer. A promotional payout is not a reliable estimate of every later payment.

What to write down from each provider

  • Total USD charged: include the fee and any known extra funding charges.
  • MXN delivered: use the final recipient amount, not just the headline rate.
  • Payment and payout methods: compare the same methods where possible.
  • Arrival estimate: check whether it meets the recipient’s deadline.
  • Quote time and promotion: note when you checked and whether the offer is limited to a first transfer.

Which provider is cheaper for Mexico?

The answer depends on the quote you qualify for, your budget and your delivery needs. Collect both quotes close together, compare the final peso payouts, then recheck the provider’s checkout screen before paying. The example above is a calculation method, not a current price ranking.

How melt value is calculated

Weight × purity × price per unit gives the estimated value of the metal. A 10-gram 18K gold item contains about 7.5 grams of gold. Stones and other materials should be excluded from the weight. Dealer payouts may include deductions for testing, refining, or resale.

Frequently asked questions

How is melt value calculated?

Multiply the item’s weight by its metal purity and the reference price per unit of weight.

What does melt value tell me?

It estimates the value of the metal content. Dealer payouts can vary with fees, refining costs, and the market price.

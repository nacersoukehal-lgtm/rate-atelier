About Rate Atelier

AboutData sourcesEditorial policyContact

Rate Atelier is an independently maintained website for people who want to understand exchange rates, estimate precious metal value, and compare their own money transfer quotes.

Why use Rate Atelier?

  • Compare transfer offers using the same total sending budget.
  • Estimate metal content from weight and purity before discussing a dealer offer.
  • Inspect dated historical observations and the source behind each reference price.
  • Use calculators in your browser without creating an account.

How we earn trust

We distinguish reference prices from offers you can transact at. Provider observation dates remain visible when a live request fails, and published snapshots retain their original dates. A new website build does not turn an old quote into a new market observation.

Read our data methodology and editorial standards. Questions and corrections go to rateatelier@gmail.com.

Our limits

Rate Atelier is an information and calculation tool, not a broker, transfer provider, or personalized investment advice service. Availability and update timing depend on third-party providers. Verify the final price, spread, and fees with your provider.
